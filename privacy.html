Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

MC_n8n_personal is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to connect the owner's own Google account to a self-hosted n8n instance. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app may access Google user data (such as Gmail, Google Calendar, or Google Drive data) belonging to the owner's own account, solely to run the owner's personal automations.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed only to perform the automations the owner has configured. It is stored only within the owner's own n8n instance and Google account, and is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred, or shared with any third party. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account permissions and can delete any stored data from the n8n instance.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the owner via github.com/myunghoonchung.
